About SoCDS

The School of Computing & Data Science (SoCDS) is a premier academic division of Centurion University of Technology & Management, Odisha. Formerly under the School of Engineering & Technology, SoCDS was established as a dedicated school to reflect the university's deep commitment to computing education and research excellence.

The school offers specialized undergraduate and postgraduate programs that blend theoretical depth with hands-on, industry-relevant training. With GPU-powered AI labs, experienced faculty, and 60+ industry MoU partners, SoCDS prepares graduates to lead in one of the world's fastest-growing sectors.

SoCDS believes in building not just skilled professionals, but critical thinkers, innovators, and responsible technologists who contribute meaningfully to society and the economy.

🔭 Our Vision

To be a globally recognized centre of excellence in computing education, research, and innovation — nurturing ethical, creative, and industry-ready technology leaders.

🎯 Our Mission

  • Deliver quality, industry-integrated computing education aligned with global standards.
  • Foster cutting-edge research, innovation, and entrepreneurial thinking among students and faculty.
  • Build ethical, inclusive, and socially responsible technology professionals for a digital society.
  • Strengthen industry, academic, and government partnerships for holistic development.
